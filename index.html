Why a clinician writer

Thirteen years of explaining things to frightened people.

Most of my working life has been spent with children who are frightened of the chair — and with the parent beside them trying to work out what to ask. Behaviour management, sedation, treatment under general anaesthesia, and the care of anxious and neurodivergent children, across Boston, Dubai and Riyadh.

The treatment nearly always went well. What kept going wrong was the explaining: leaflets nobody could follow, consent material that assumed you already had the vocabulary, education modules written by people who had never had to deliver the message out loud.

So I started writing it properly. Cedar Science Studio is where that work lives — health content that's honest about the evidence and still makes sense on a first read. I'm also an Advanced Certified Autism Specialist, which shapes how I write for families whose children process information differently.

I'm taking on new clients. If you're a healthcare brand, a dental company or an educational organisation that needs a writer who understands both the science and the patient in the chair, I'd like to hear about it.
